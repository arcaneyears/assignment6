Task 11. Image Gallery

Part 5. Mini Project

Astana

How it works

  • A click on a thumbnail fades the large picture out with .fadeOut().
  • In the callback the src and the caption are replaced with .attr() and .text().
  • Then the picture is shown again with .fadeIn().
  • The thumbnails grow a little on mouseenter and go back on mouseleave.
  • The Previous and Next buttons walk through the same list.
