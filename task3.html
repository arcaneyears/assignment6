Task 3. Fade Methods

Part 2. jQuery Effects

How it works

.fadeOut() animates the opacity down to zero and then hides the element, .fadeIn() does the opposite, and .fadeToggle() chooses one of them depending on the current state. .fadeTo() stops at a given opacity instead.
